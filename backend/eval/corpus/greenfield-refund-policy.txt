Greenfield Outfitters Refund Policy, version 1 (synthetic sample document).

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the returned item is inspected. Sale items marked final sale cannot be returned. Return shipping is free for members of the Greenfield Club; other customers pay a flat 6 dollar return fee.
